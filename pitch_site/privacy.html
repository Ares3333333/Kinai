Privacy passport

Privacy is part of the product. Camera frames are processed locally; the server receives only numeric derived signals. Audio is not recorded. Consent is written to the audit log.

Exact promises

  1. Raw video / audio. Frames stay on the device and are not stored.
  2. What is sent. Only derived signals: tilt/readiness/recovery, tension markers, and labels.
  3. Consent. Initial consent is logged in data/consent_log.jsonl.
  4. Not medical. Kinaesthetic AI is performance coaching, not diagnosis or treatment.
