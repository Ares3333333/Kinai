Protocol

  1. Open the session page and accept the privacy prompt.
  2. Start the live engine or use browser demo mode.
  3. Sit so your face and shoulders are visible. Front light is best.
  4. Save baseline: neutral, jaw lock, shoulders, release.
  5. Play normally for 5 minutes.
  6. During the session, press one key:
    • H - command helped
    • T - I felt tension
    • F - false alert
  7. After the game, fill the short form on the right.
